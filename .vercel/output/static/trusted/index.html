People we read

Being listed here is not an endorsement of everything someone argues. The list deliberately includes people who disagree with each other, because on this subject the disagreement is the most useful thing to understand.

  • Sam Gregory

    Executive Director, WITNESS

    Deepfakes, synthetic media and how anyone can tell what is real.

  • Jonathan Haidt

    Social psychologist, NYU Stern

    Argues that a play-based childhood gave way to a phone-based one. The most influential case for restriction, and the most contested.

  • Tristan Harris

    Co-founder, Center for Humane Technology

    How products are designed to hold attention, explained clearly enough to repeat to a child.

  • J. Nathan Matias

    Cornell, Citizens and Technology Lab

    Studies how platforms are governed, and runs research communities can actually participate in.

  • Catherine Price

    Author, How to Break Up With Your Phone

    Practical habit change for adults, which is where most family screen problems actually start.

  • Science editorial desk

    Peer-reviewed journal

    Useful counterweight on how unsettled the social media research actually is.
