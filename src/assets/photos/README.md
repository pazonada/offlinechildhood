# Photos

Original photographs by the site owner. Drop full-size files straight in here;
they are optimised at build time into static files, so they cost nothing to
serve and never touch a metered image service.

Only use photographs in which no child is identifiable. The site tells parents
to ask before posting pictures of their children, and to notice that adults post
their kids publicly for years and then ask those kids to be careful online. The
illustrations should survive that advice.

## Two exceptions on record

`classroom.jpg` is not an original. It is an Unsplash photograph by Taylor
Flowe, licensed for commercial use with no attribution required, standing in on
the teachers page until the owner takes one. Replace it when that happens.

`robot.jpg` shows the owner's own daughter, whose face is visible in profile.
That is a deliberate call by the person entitled to make it. The rule above
still holds for everyone else's children, which is why a seventh photograph was
deleted rather than published.
