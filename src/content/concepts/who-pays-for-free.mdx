## The one that makes the others cheap

Once a child knows who is paying, they can work out most of the other concepts on
this site for themselves rather than being told.

Why does it autoplay? Because a stopped session earns nothing. Why the streak?
Because returning tomorrow is worth more than today. Why does it want the
notification permission so badly? Because a session it can restart is worth more
than one it cannot.

None of that needs to be memorised once the incentive is understood.

## Do not make it a morality tale

The companies are not cartoon villains, and framing them that way is both
inaccurate and easy for a teenager to dismiss.

They are organisations responding to the incentives they have. The people
building these products are mostly ordinary and many are uneasy about the result.
What matters is that the incentive does not point at your child's interests, and
that no amount of good intention inside the company changes the direction the
incentive points.

That framing survives contact with a sceptical fifteen-year-old. The villain
version does not.
