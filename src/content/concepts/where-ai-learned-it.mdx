## Why children get this faster than adults

Every child has been taught, repeatedly, that you ask before using someone
else's things and that copying someone's work and calling it yours is wrong.
Those rules are enforced daily at school.

So when you explain that a company built a product by absorbing millions of
people's work without asking, they do not need the argument explained. They already have the framework. Their instinct is usually sharper than the
adult version.

## Let them disagree with you

There is a real argument on the other side, and a child who only hears one half
will be poorly equipped the first time they meet the other.

Humans do learn by absorbing other people's work. Every writer read first. The
question is whether doing it mechanically, at enormous scale, for commercial
gain, is the same act or a different one. That is a hard question and
smart people land in different places.

Say so. A kid who knows this is contested will keep thinking about it. A kid who was
handed a verdict might stop thinking about it.
