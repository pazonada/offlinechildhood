## The rule that matters more than the rest

One thing on this page does more work than everything else: your child has to
believe that telling you will make it better rather than worse.

Every one of these harms depends on silence. Sextortion depends on it completely.
The scam only works while the child thinks they will be blamed, punished, or have
their devices taken away.

This means the most protective thing you can do is boring – say, repeatedly and
in advance, that if anything goes wrong they can tell you and you will not be
angry and you will not confiscate anything as a punishment. Then, when something
small goes wrong, prove it by not being angry. The small incidents are the
rehearsal for the big one.

## Why fear does not work here

Frightening children about strangers has been tried extensively and it has two problems: it aims attention at the
rarest category of harm, and it makes children
hide ordinary trouble because they expect an alarmed reaction.

Verification works better. Get them to ask: "Who is this? How would I know?
What are they asking me to do? Does it involve hurrying or hiding?" Those
questions apply to a stranger as well as to somebody they know, which matters,
because statistically the latter is far more likely to be the problem.
